How review works

  1. You submit the statement.
  2. The cadet's platoon commander approves and forwards it, returns it for correction, or rejects it.
  3. The company commander gives the final decision.

Only approved statements affect the cadet's discipline grade. You can't review your own statements.

Every step is recorded with the reviewer's name, time and reason.
