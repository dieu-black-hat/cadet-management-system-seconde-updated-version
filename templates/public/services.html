Features

What each part of the system provides, and who uses it.

Accounts and roles

Administrator-created accounts with single-use set-password links, six roles, per-user extra permissions, suspension and deactivation.

Used by: administrators

Cadet records

Registration, photographs, intake/company/platoon assignment with history, CSV import and export, archiving instead of deletion.

Used by: administrators, all staff (read)

Disciplinary statements

Drafts, evidence uploads, submission, correction and resubmission, with a full decision history.

Used by: instructors

Review queues

Pending items with waiting time and overdue flags; approve, return or reject with a reason.

Used by: platoon and company commanders

Discipline grading

Configurable bands, colours and deductions; versioned policies; an itemised explanation for every grade; audited overrides.

Used by: administrators, commanders

Medical module

Restricted records and private documents; commanders see only a released fitness summary.

Used by: doctors

Reports

Trends, workflow statistics and grade distribution for a chosen period, with PDF and CSV export.

Used by: commanders, administrators, auditors

Notifications

In-app alerts for submissions, decisions and returns; optional e-mail with no sensitive details.

Used by: everyone

Audit trail

Sign-ins, permission changes, decisions, downloads and exports — append-only.

Used by: administrators, auditors
