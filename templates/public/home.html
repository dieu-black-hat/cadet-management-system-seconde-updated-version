From incident to decision

The same four steps for every statement, enforced by the server — no step can be skipped and nobody can approve their own report.

1. Instructor reports

Facts, category, severity and evidence, saved as a draft until ready.

2. Platoon commander reviews

Approve and forward, return for correction, or reject — with a reason.

3. Company commander decides

Final approval within the company's authority, recorded with name and time.

4. Grade is recalculated

Only approved statements count, under a versioned, explainable policy.

Built for the people who use it

Each role sees only its own units: instructors their cadets, commanders their platoon or company, doctors the medical records they are responsible for. Medical details never reach a commander's screen — only an approved fitness summary does.
