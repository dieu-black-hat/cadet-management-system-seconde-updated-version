# User guide

## Everyone
* **Sign in** with the e-mail and password set from your invitation link. Five wrong attempts lock the
  account for 15 minutes. Use *Forgot password?* to get a new single-use link.
* The **bell** shows notifications; clicking one opens the record (access is checked again).
* **Search** (top bar) finds cadets and statement references within your units.
* **My profile** shows your units and exactly what you are allowed to do.

## Instructor
1. *New statement* → choose the cadet, describe facts (date, place, what happened), choose category and
   severity, optionally attach evidence (PDF/JPG/PNG/DOCX, 5 MB).
2. *Save as draft* or *Save and submit*. You can't change a statement while it is in review.
3. If it is **returned**, correct it and *Resubmit*. You can withdraw your own statement with a reason.

## Platoon commander
*Review queue* lists statements from your platoon. Open one, then **Approve & forward**, **Return for
correction** or **Reject**. A reason is required to return or reject. Overdue items are flagged.

## Company commander
Gives the **final approval**, return or rejection for statements forwarded from the company's platoons.
Only approved statements change a cadet's grade. Can record a manual override (with reason) on a cadet
page, and export reports.

## Doctor
*Medical workspace* → records for cadets in your units. Clinical notes and documents are visible only to
medical staff. The **summary approved for command** and fitness status are the only details commanders
see — keep diagnoses out of them.

## Administrator
* *Users & roles*: invite users, change role, suspend/deactivate, grant extra permissions, **assign the
  user's company or platoon** (without an assignment a user sees no cadets).
* *Organisation*: intakes, companies, platoons. *Cadet directory*: register, edit, reassign (history is
  kept), archive, import/export CSV.
* *Grading policy*: create a new version, edit bands/colours/deductions, activate (all grades are
  recalculated; history kept).
* *System settings*: company review on/off, overdue threshold, e-mail notifications.
* *Audit trail*: every sign-in, decision, permission change, download and export.

## Auditor
Read-only access to the audit trail, reports and directories; no medical access.
